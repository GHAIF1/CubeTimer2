        About

        CubeTimer is a simple, clean stopwatch built for speedcubers. Press Space to start a solve and press it again to stop — every finished solve is saved automatically, so you can watch your progress over time and even compare your pace with the official world record.

        Developer

        CubeTimer was created by Rubik’s Cube enthusiasts Cassiel Stössel and Quirin Leumann. Cassiel developed the website, while Quirin created the concept. Together, they built a simple and reliable tool for speedcubers to track solves, follow progress, and improve their performance.
